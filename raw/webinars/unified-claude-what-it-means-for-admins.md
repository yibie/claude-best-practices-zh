# Unified Claude: What it means for admins
Chat and Cowork are coming together into a single, simpler Claude experience across web, desktop, and mobile: one place for everything from asking a quick question to producing a deliverable with a full analysis.

We invite admins and security teams to join Anthropic’s team for a look at what’s changing for your organization, and when. Leave knowing why one unified Claude is simpler for your team and easier for you to govern, and exactly how to make the switch. We’ll leave time for Q&A at the end.
