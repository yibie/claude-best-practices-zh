# Building with Claude in Europe: Agent Fundamentals
Claude Resources

On demand

Join Anthropic and Mesh-AI for a technical deep-dive into building production-ready AI agents with Claude. Learn the fundamentals of agentic AI, explore real-world deployments across European customers, and discover how to evaluate and deploy agents safely, at scale.

## Featured speakers

-   

    Adriaan Engelbrecht

    Applied AI @ Anthropic

-   

    Steve Bryen

    CTO @ Mesh-AI


## What you’ll learn

-   The difference between workflows and agents, the philosophical foundations of “agency”, and what makes truly agentic systems distinct from traditional automation.

-   The technical fundamentals of agent development through hands-on demos.

-   Practical approaches for assessing agentic capabilities and ensuring your agents meet performance benchmarks.

-   How you can leverage Claude Agents in production.

-   Insights from customer deployments across Europe, covering deployment patterns, regulatory considerations, and measurable outcomes.
