# Claude Cowork product guide
Most AI tools are conversational. You ask a question in a chat window, you get an answer back, and the work of turning that answer into something—a deck, a doc, a spreadsheet, a sent email—is still yours.

Claude Cowork is built for the work, not just the answer. It's a version of Claude that reads and writes files on your computer, uses your connected work apps, and runs multi-step tasks on your behalf. This guide is for analysts, PMs, operators, researchers, marketers, recruiters, and anyone else whose day is spent moving information between systems.
